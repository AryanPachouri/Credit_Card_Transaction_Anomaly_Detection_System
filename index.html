Anomaly detection in credit card transactions

A multivariate Gaussian density model, fitted on the 9 features that best separate authentic from fraudulent transactions in the ULB credit card fraud dataset. A transaction is flagged when its joint density falls below a threshold tuned for F2-score. All computation below runs client-side, in your browser — nothing is sent to a server.
